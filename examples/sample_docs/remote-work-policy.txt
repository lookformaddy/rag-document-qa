Remote Work Policy (fictional sample document)

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, provided their manager approves the schedule in advance. Remote days must be recorded in the team calendar by Monday morning. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone, during which all team members must be reachable. Equipment for home offices, including a monitor and headset, may be requested through the IT portal once per year. Employees working remotely more than three days per week require written approval from both their manager and the head of people operations.
