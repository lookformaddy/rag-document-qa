Order Refund Policy (fictional sample document)

Standard orders may be refunded within thirty days of delivery, provided the items are unused and in their original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five to seven business days of approval. Custom or personalized orders are final sale and cannot be refunded, though they may be exchanged within fourteen days if they arrive damaged. Shipping fees are non-refundable except when the order arrives damaged or incorrect. To request a refund, customers submit the order number through the support portal and select the items to return.
